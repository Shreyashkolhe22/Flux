HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Then never again.

Pair once with a QR code. After that, sending is a tap.

Install

Run the Windows installer. Install the Android app. Flux opens the firewall for your local network by itself.

Pair once

Scan the QR code shown on your laptop. A random key is saved on both devices, and nothing else needs setting up.

Send

Type, paste, share from any app, or tap the widget. Text and links land in your clipboard. Files land in a folder.

FEATURES

Small tool. Careful details.

Ready to paste

Text and links go straight to your laptop clipboard and a notification says so. Press Ctrl + V anywhere.

Images and files

Photos and PDFs, documents, zips up to 100 MB. They are streamed to disk and saved in Downloads\Flux. Nothing is ever overwritten.

Share from any app

Flux is in the Android Share sheet. Send a link from Chrome, a photo from Gallery, a file from Files.

Widget and quick tile

Copy something, tap the home-screen widget or the pull-down tile. One tap, no app to open.

Offline by design

Works on your phone hotspot with no internet at all. Laptop off? Items wait and send themselves when it is back.

Private by default

No account to make, no analytics, no tracking. What you send stays between your two devices.

Pair once, find it again

If your laptop gets a new address, Flux searches your network and reconnects. A key from the QR code is required on every send, so other devices on the network cannot use it.

FAQ

Good questions.

Does Flux need the internet?

No. Your phone talks to your laptop directly over your Wi-Fi or hotspot. Nothing goes to a server.

Is it secure?

Every send needs a random key from the QR code, so other devices on the network cannot use your laptop. The connection itself is not encrypted, so use Flux on a network you trust, like your own hotspot or home Wi-Fi, not on public Wi-Fi.

Does Flux collect any data?

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. What you send stays between your two devices.

Does it work on iPhone or Mac?

Not yet. Flux is Android to Windows for now.

What is the file size limit?

100 MB per file. Photos are compressed to JPEG before sending.

Where do received files go?

Into Downloads\Flux on your laptop. Text and links go to the clipboard instead. Flux never opens a received file for you.

Why does Android say "pasted from your clipboard"?

That is Android's own privacy notice, shown whenever an app reads the clipboard. The widget and tile need to read it to send.

SETUP GUIDE

From download to first send.

About five minutes. You need a Windows laptop and an Android phone.

  1. Install Flux on your laptop

    1. Download the Windows installer and double-click it.
    2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
    3. Click Yes when asked for permission. This lets Flux open one firewall port for your local network.
    4. Flux starts, a window with a QR code appears, and a small Flux icon sits in the tray at the bottom right. Leave the QR window open.

  2. Install Flux on your phone

    1. Download the Android app on your phone, or send the file to it.
    2. Tap the file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager, then tap Install.
    3. Open Flux.

  3. Join the same network

    1. Easiest: turn on your phone hotspot and connect the laptop to it. It works with no internet at all.
    2. Or: connect both devices to the same home Wi-Fi.

    Heads up: some office, school and guest networks stop devices talking to each other. If pairing fails there, use your phone hotspot instead.

  4. Pair with the QR code

    1. In the Flux app, tap Scan QR code and allow the camera.
    2. Point it at the QR code on your laptop screen.
    3. The app shows a green CONNECTED and your laptop's name. You only do this once.
    4. Lost the QR window? Click the Flux icon in the laptop tray to show it again.

  5. Send something

    1. Type or paste in the box and tap the red arrow.
    2. Share from any app: tap Share, then Flux.
    3. Image, Camera or File: pick it and it is sent.
    4. Widget or tile: in Flux open Settings, then Shortcuts, and add them. Copy anything, tap once.

    On the laptop you get a notification. Text and links are already in your clipboard, so just press Ctrl + V. Images and files are in Downloads\Flux. Click the notification to show the file.

If something does not work

The phone says NO SIGNAL or cannot find the laptop

Both devices must be on the same network and Flux must be running on the laptop (look for the tray icon). Try the phone hotspot. If you installed from source and not with the installer, the firewall rule may be missing. See the GitHub page for the one command.

It works in my phone's browser but not in the app

Turn mobile data off on the phone and try again. Flux sends over Wi-Fi, but some phones route oddly on a Wi-Fi without internet.

I changed networks, or the key was reset

In the app open Devices, tap Forget, then scan the QR code again. To get a new key on the laptop, right-click the tray icon and choose Reset pairing key.

Port 3000 is in use

Another program is using the port Flux needs. Close it and start Flux again.
