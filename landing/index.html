03 / SETUP

Five
minutes.

You need a Windows laptop and an Android phone. After this, sending is a tap.

1

LAPTOP

  • Download the installer and run it.
  • If Windows says "protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Click Yes. A window with a QR code opens. Leave it open.
2

PHONE

  • Download the app on your phone.
  • Open the file. If asked, allow Install unknown apps, then Install.
  • Open Flux, tap Scan QR code, and point it at the laptop screen.
3

SEND

  • Put both on the same network. Easiest: your phone hotspot.
  • Type, paste, Share from any app, or tap the widget.
  • On the laptop press Ctrl + V. Files are in Downloads\Flux.

Office, school or guest Wi-Fi can block devices from talking to each other. If pairing fails there, use your phone hotspot. More help is in the questions below.

05 / QUESTIONS

Good
questions.

Does Flux need the internet?

No. Your phone talks to your laptop directly over your Wi-Fi or hotspot. Nothing goes to a server.

Is it secure?

Every send needs a random key from the QR code, so other devices on the network cannot use your laptop. The connection itself is not encrypted, so use Flux on a network you trust, like your own hotspot or home Wi-Fi, not on public Wi-Fi.

Does it collect any data?

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. What you send stays between your two devices.

The phone says NO SIGNAL or cannot find the laptop

Both devices must be on the same network and Flux must be running on the laptop (look for the tray icon). Try your phone hotspot. If it works in the phone's browser but not in the app, turn mobile data off and try again.

I changed networks, or reset the key

In the app open Devices, tap Forget, then scan the QR code again. To get a new key, right-click the laptop tray icon and choose Reset pairing key. To see the QR again, click the tray icon.

Where do received files go?

Into Downloads\Flux on your laptop. Text and links go to the clipboard instead. Flux never opens a received file for you. The limit is 100 MB per file.

Why does Android say "pasted from your clipboard"?

That is Android's own privacy notice, shown whenever an app reads the clipboard. The widget and tile need to read it to send.

Does it work on iPhone or Mac?

Not yet. Flux is Android to Windows for now.
